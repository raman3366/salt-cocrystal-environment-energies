### Question

Near ΔpKa ≈ 0 the ΔpKa rule cannot tell salt from cocrystal. How much does the crystal lower the proton-transfer energy
relative to the isolated pair, and which part supplies it: the long-range lattice field or the first hydrogen-bond
shell? Tested on one protocol across a ΔpKa series with positive and negative controls.

optc/crystals.json (CIFs in optc/cif/, md5 in optc/cif/MD5SUMS). Core: cyano (2223527), saph (2239894), carboxy
(2213511), chloro (2017559), anis (2003440), perchlorate (2238404, positive control), dapsone (2233792, regular negative
control), mppcp (7110891, short-bond negative control, neutron 30 K), brsalt / brcoc (7232328 / 7232332, polymorph pair,
ranking only). Optional, run if the budget allows: sulfamate (7230795), clsalt (7232329); nitrate (2231831) only with a
primitive-cell builder. ΔpKa values with their sources are in crystals.json; the dapsone, pentachlorophenol and
4-bromo-2-methylaniline values are to be fixed from primary sources before any result is read.

### Structures (gate G1, optc/src/cell.py)

- Symmetry operations from the CIF's own loop; sites merged below 0.05 Å; covalent bonds d < r_i + r_j + 0.40 Å
  (Cordero 2008), each H on its nearest heavy atom; charges from the molecular formula (species in crystals.json).
- Hydrogen bonds: H···A < 2.60 Å, ∠D–H···A > 110°, A not within two bonds of D.

  ties (< 0.005 Å) by the larger angle, then CIF order. Runner-up within 0.10 Å recorded (R1f repeated on it).
- Collective transfer: every copy of the chosen bond in the cell (same CIF labels).

  13 on 18 Oct 2026, max |Δd| = 0).

### Periodic rung R5 (optc/src/qe.py)

- QE 7.5 (conda-forge build h19104ac_2), PBE-D3(BJ) two-body, SSSP PBE efficiency 1.3.0 at 70/560 Ry, fixed X-ray cell,
  unshifted Monkhorst–Pack mesh n_i = ceil(9.0 Å / |a_i|); X–H of X-ray structures started at neutron means.
- P0 = S, the observed state, BFGS (forces 5e-4 Ry/bohr, energy 1e-5 Ry).
- P4 = the other end: every copy of the transferring H held at r_eq from A, where r_eq is the A–H length of the isolated
  receiving partner with the proton (PBE-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVP, optc/src/mol.py req). P1–P3: d(D–H) = d_S + k/4 (L_S − r_eq
  − d_S), k = 1, 2, 3 (d_S, L_S = mean D–H and D···A at S). Damped dynamics with pw.x distance constraints (forces 1e-3,
  energy 1e-4 Ry), as the SAPH paper's profile. Seeds: S with the H moved on the D→A line; points independent.
- PBE0-D3(BJ) at P0–P4: SG15 ONCV v1.2 at 100/400 Ry, q mesh 1×1×1, path(PBE0) = path(PBE/SSSP) + [E(PBE0) − E(PBE)]_SG15.
- Checks: the denser mesh (n_i + 1) at P0 and P4; another proton changing its donor along the path is reported.
- Energies per formula unit (all copies move: per cell / number of copies).
- The PBE0 electrostatic potential at P0 and P4 (pp.x, V_bare + V_H) is kept for the periodic charge fit.

### Molecular rungs (code and validation rules to be fixed here before their first production run)

R1f pair at the crystal geometry; R4a pair in the lattice field; R3 pair + first shell; R4 R3 in the field; PySCF
PBE0-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVP. Pair correction: DF-MP2 and ωB97X-D minus PBE0 on R1f at P0–P4; final class = minimum of the
corrected periodic profile, "shared" if within 0.1 Å of the midpoint. PCM analogue (secondary). Bridge G2b: PySCF and
QE pair ΔE within 4 kJ/mol on saph and mppcp.
